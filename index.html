Festival overview

This four-day parish festival welcomes neighbors and returning families with Orthodox hospitality to enjoy carnival attractions, local food vendors, and live music performances.

Festival dates

Thursday, June 4 through Sunday, July 7

Location

St. Mary Antiochian Orthodox Church, Palos Heights, Illinois

Attractions

Carnival rides, live music, and great food, all in a mix of local and exotic flavors

Admission

Adults $5; Children Free (5 and under)
