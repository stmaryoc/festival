Schedule

Festival Program at a Glance

Our schedule is full of wholesame activities for the whole family: carnival rides, live bands, church tours, and more!

Thursday: Carnival Opening (June 4)

  1. 5:00 PM

    Thursday opening

    Our parish grounds open for our carnival and food/drink options.

  2. 10:00 PM

    Thursday closing

    Our parish grounds close for the day.

Friday: Church Opening (June 5)

  1. 5:00 PM

    Friday opening

    In addition to the carnival and meal options, our church opens to tours throughout the day.

  2. 6:00 PM

    Byzantine choir concert

    Enjoy the St. Mary choir as they chant sacred hymns within the church building.

  3. 8:00 PM

    Stand-up comedy with Vito Zatto

    Kick back for some family-friendly laughs with renowned Chicago comedian Vito Zatto as he performs a stand-up routine with cover songs.

  4. 10:30 PM

    Friday close

    Our parish grounds close for the day.

Saturday: Activity Day (June 6)

  1. 12:00 PM

    Saturday open

    Our parish grounds open to all of our offerings: carnival, food/drink, and church tours.

  2. 2:00 PM

    Fire Truck Visit

    Meet and greet with our local fire department as they give fire truck rides for the whole family!

  3. 3:00 PM

    Live Concert: How Rude!

    Take a trip down memory lane with Chicago's favorite 90's tribute band as they play the classics.

  4. 7:00 PM

    Live Concert: Noor Kurdi & Saed Khoury

    Enjoy St. Mary's very own Saed & Noor as they sings us through a set of some of the parish's favorite Arabic tunes.

  5. 10:00 PM

    Saturday close

    Our parish grounds close for the day.

Sunday: Encore & Closing (June 7)

  1. 12:00 PM

    Sunday open

    Our parish grounds open to all of our offerings: carnival, food/drink, and church tours.

  2. 3:00 PM

    Live Concert: Buckle

    Saddle up and strap on your cowboy boots for Buckle, one of Chicago's hottest country-rock bands!

  3. 6:30 PM

    Live Concert: Saed Khoury & Bara Talahmeh

    An encore concert of Arabic music with Saed & Bara!

  4. 9:00 PM

    Sunday closing

    The conclusion of the festival; we hope to see you next year!
