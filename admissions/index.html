Admissions

Simple Admission for the Whole Family

Admission is collected once per day and supports festival operations and parish ministries.

Admission tiers

Adults

$5 entry fee

Valid for one day of festival entry (carnival not included)

Children

5 and under free

No separate admission pass required at entry

What admission includes

  • Live stage music throughout the day
  • Access to food and drink vendors, including a beer tent
  • Guided church access, including tours and the Byzantine concert

Purchase admission through the parish portal

If online purchasing is unavailable, gate entry remains available by card or cash.
