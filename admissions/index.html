Admissions

Simple Admissions for the Whole Family

All entry fees are collected at the festival gate each day and support festival operations and parish ministries.

Admission tiers

Adults

$5 entry fee

Valid for one day of festival entry (cash only)

Children

5 and under free

No separate admission pass required at entry

What admission includes

  • Entry with small bags, reusable water bottles (empty), and compact cameras
  • Live stage music throughout the day
  • Access to food and drink vendors, including a beer tent
  • Guided church access, including tours and the Byzantine concert

All entry fees are collected at the festival gate each day with no re-entry permitted.

Gate admissions can be purchased by cash only.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Are children allowed at the festival?
A: The festival is intended for all ages, and any adult-only areas (such as the beer tent) are strictly monitored by festival staff.
Q: Can I bring X into the festival?
A: Small bags, empty reusable water bottles, and compact cameras are allowed; professional photo/video equipment, outside alcohol, weapons, and smoking or illegal substances are prohibited.
Q: Will the festival be held in the event of bad weather?
A: The festival operates rain or shine unless safety is at risk, and cancellations or major changes are announced on the website and church social channels.
